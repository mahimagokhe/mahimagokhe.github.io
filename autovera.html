Case study · Mobile product experience

AutoVera

A premium automotive showroom and service companion that lets customers discover vehicles, save favorites, book maintenance, and track service from one focused mobile workflow.

ReactLucide iconsResponsive mobile UIProduct UX
The product challenge

Vehicle discovery and after-sales service often live in separate experiences. AutoVera brings both journeys together, making it easy to move from browsing a product to booking, tracking, and managing its service lifecycle.

What I built

The supplied React prototype models a complete customer journey inside a polished mobile viewport, from first launch and onboarding through authentication, showroom discovery, product comparison, wishlist management, service booking, notifications, and profile actions.

Explore

Searchable showroom, category chips, featured products, offers, image galleries, specifications, ratings, colors, and similar products.

Own

Wishlist state, service history, notifications, profile navigation, booking details, invoices, and contact points support the ownership lifecycle.

Service

A seven-step booking flow covers vehicle, service, center, date, time, issue details, pickup/drop-off, and confirmation.

End-to-end experience

01 · First launchAnimated splash, three-step onboarding, and a focused authentication surface establish brand and intent before the main app.
02 · DiscoveryHome highlights showroom, service, featured products, offers, quick actions, and upcoming service context.
03 · DecisionProduct detail combines gallery navigation, wishlist state, pricing, reviews, color selection, technical specifications, and similar products.
04 · CareService center discovery, booking progress, tracking timeline, history filters, and booking details make post-purchase support actionable.

Frontend engineering decisions

Reusable UI primitives

Shared atoms such as Btn, Chip, Stars, SectionHeader, InfoRow, SelectCard, and SummaryRow keep visual and interaction behavior consistent across screens.

Stateful navigation

The app keeps screen, tab, stack, payload, wishlist, and booking state separate. This makes forward navigation, back navigation, deep product details, and booking completion predictable within a single-page prototype.

Interaction quality

Lucide icons, active states, disabled progression, animated transitions, horizontal offer/product rails, filtered history, image pagination, and clear success/tracking states give the prototype a product-level feel.

Production path

Mock data is isolated in collections such as PRODUCTS, SERVICE_CENTERS, and BOOKING_HISTORY, making it straightforward to replace the demo layer with API services, authentication, and persistent user data.

Why it represents experienced frontend work

AutoVera demonstrates more than a visual mobile mockup: it combines design-system thinking, multi-step stateful workflows, navigation history, reusable React components, responsive interaction patterns, accessibility-minded controls, and a clear boundary between demo data and future services.
